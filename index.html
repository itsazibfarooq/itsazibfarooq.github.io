Azib Farooq

PhD Student, University of Central Florida, Orlando - MS from Miami University, Ohio - BSc from UET Taxila, Pakistan.

Orlando, Florida, USA

Hi, I am Azib Farooq, a PhD student in Computer Engineering at the University of Central Florida (transferred from the University of Cincinnati), where I am a Graduate Trustee Fellow. Previously, I completed my MS in Computer Science at Miami University on a fully funded scholarship, and my BSc in Electrical Engineering at UET Taxila, Pakistan.

My current research interests include vision-language-action (VLA) models, world models, and reinforcement learning. I work on making VLA inference faster through action-aware visual token reduction for flow-matching (pi0, pi0.5) and autoregressive action decoders, and on using learned video world models to generate synthetic manipulation trajectories so that robot policies depend less on teleoperated demonstrations.

Previously, I worked on diffusion-based 3D and 4D scene editing (NeRF, 3D Gaussian Splatting), efficient on-device inference (quantization, pruning, distillation, TensorRT) as a Research Assistant at the Procter & Gamble Digital Accelerator, and on code-repair and tutoring agents as a Machine Learning Engineer at AlgoLab.

I serve as a reviewer for AAAI and IJCNN.
