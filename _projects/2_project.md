- Designed a training-free framework for text-driven editing of dynamic 3D (4D) scenes, enforcing spatio-temporal consistency through inter- and intra-view correlated noise sampling in a pretrained text-to-image diffusion model.
- Repurposed a 2D diffusion prior as a plug-and-play 4D editor for style transfer, localized editing, and object removal, with no per-scene optimization or fine-tuning.
- Published as *PSF-4D* at SMI 2026.
