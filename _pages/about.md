<b>Hi!</b> I am <b>Azib Farooq</b>, a <b>Ph.D. student in Computer Engineering</b> at the <a href="https://www.ucf.edu/">University of Central Florida</a> (transferred from the University of Cincinnati), where I am a <span class="hl">Graduate Trustee Fellow</span> advised by <a href="https://www.ece.ucf.edu/person/nazanin-rahnavard/"><b>Dr. Nazanin Rahnavard</b></a> and <a href="https://www.ece.ucf.edu/person/mingyo-seo/"><b>Dr. Mingyo Seo</b></a>. Previously, I completed my <b>M.S. in Computer Science</b> at <a href="https://miamioh.edu/">Miami University</a> on a <u>fully funded scholarship</u> (GPA 4.00/4.00), where I worked with <a href="https://miamioh.edu/profiles/cec/computer-science-software-engineering/_documents/curriculum-vitae-september-26.pdf"><b>Dr. Eric Bachmann</b></a>, and my <b>B.Sc. in Electrical Engineering</b> at UET Taxila, Pakistan.

<b>My current research interests</b> include <span class="hl">Diffusion based robust trajectory planning</span>, <span class="hl">unhackable RL agent environment generation</span>, and <span class="hl">reinforcement learning</span>. I work to integrate contact rich representation to enhance previously developed trajectory generation capability via solely relying on vision and geometry for robotic systems. 

<b>Previously</b>, I worked on <b>diffusion-based 3D and 4D scene editing</b> (NeRF, 3D Gaussian Splatting), published at venues including <span class="venue">ECCV</span>, <span class="venue">ACL</span>, and <span class="venue">Information Fusion</span>. I also worked on <b>efficient on-device inference</b> (quantization, pruning, distillation, TensorRT) as a Research Assistant at the <b>Procter &amp; Gamble Digital Accelerator</b>, and on code-repair and tutoring agents as a Machine Learning Engineer at <b>AlgoLab</b>.

I serve as a <b>reviewer</b> for <span class="venue">AAAI 2026/2027</span> and <span class="venue">IJCNN 2026</span>.
